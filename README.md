# 🩸 Sanjeevani

### Blood & Plasma Supply Coordination Platform

Sanjeevani is a digital platform designed to improve blood inventory management, reduce blood wastage, and improve coordination between hospitals and blood banks.

The MVP connects hospital/blood-bank workers and donors through a simple digital interface.

---

## 🎯 Problem

India faces a shortage of blood, but the problem is not only insufficient donation.

Blood units can also be wasted because of:

- Poor inventory visibility
- Blood expiry
- Hospitals managing inventory independently
- Difficulty finding compatible blood during emergencies
- Inefficient coordination between healthcare organizations

Sanjeevani aims to improve utilization of existing blood inventory while keeping blood donation and supply within legal and medical protocols.

---

## 💡 Solution

Sanjeevani provides a digital coordination layer for hospitals and blood banks.

Blood-bank workers can register individual blood units using:

- Unique serial number
- Blood group
- Donation date
- Expiry date

The system automatically aggregates these units into hospital inventory.

The platform can then provide:

- Inventory visibility
- Expiry monitoring
- Emergency blood requests
- High-alert inventory information
- Donor appointment management
- Logistics coordination
- AI-powered inventory recommendations

Sanjeevani does **not sell or trade blood**.

---

## 👥 User Portals

### Hospital / Blood Bank Portal

Hospital and blood-bank workers can:

- View blood inventory
- Register new blood units
- Track individual blood units
- Monitor approaching expiry
- Create emergency requests
- View logistics coordination
- View donor appointments
- Monitor high-alert blood supply
- Get AI-based inventory recommendations

### Donor Portal

Donors can:

- Book blood donation appointments
- Book home collection
- Maintain donation information
